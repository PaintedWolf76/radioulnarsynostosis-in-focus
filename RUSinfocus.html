What is RUS (Radioulnar Synostosis)?

Medical Version: RUS is a bone condition in which the radial and ulnar bones of the elbow are partially or fully fused, preventing full pronation or supination of the hand.

Layman’s Version: RUS prevents one from turning their palm fully face up or down because two bones in the elbow are fused together to a certain extent. It can be both congenital or caused by physical trauma.
